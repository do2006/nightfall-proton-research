# Community Launch Copy

## Developer / research post

NightFall Technologies has published Proton Public Research Release 1.0.

Proton is our research integration around persistent governed cognition: recurring experience can qualify into a bounded candidate capability, traverse counterfactual analysis and multiple proof classes, then move through authorized transactional promotion with rollback and quarantine controls retained afterward.

We are not open-sourcing the proprietary Proton/MCF/CSC engine. Instead, the repository publishes the claims, machine-readable evidence summaries, integrity hashes, black-box evaluation specification, demonstration protocol, and limitations so the work can be challenged without handing out the implementation.

Repository: https://github.com/do2006/nightfall-proton-research
Technical progress: https://www.nightfalltechnologies.com/progress#nmmq-implementations

Feedback on the evaluation design and falsifiable claims is welcome.
