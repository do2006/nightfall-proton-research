# Public Demonstration Protocol

The public demonstration is designed to reveal behavior without revealing implementation.

## Sequence

1. Record a clean runtime/checkpoint identifier and evaluator version.
2. Exercise a bounded task before the target learned capability is available.
3. Present independently identified recurring successful observations.
4. Show that insufficient recurrence remains untrusted.
5. Show the candidate and its externally visible task/resource boundary.
6. Present the configured proof-class outcomes without exposing proprietary proof-generation internals.
7. Record authorization and transactional promotion identifiers.
8. Re-run the applicable task and record activation.
9. Exercise an excluded/out-of-boundary case and record refusal/non-activation.
10. Inject a permitted synthetic drift/corruption fixture and record quarantine.
11. Demonstrate rollback/recovery to the prior trusted checkpoint.
12. Export hashes and sanitized transcripts for independent comparison.

No production credentials, private signing material, proprietary source, model weights, or internal databases may appear in the recording or exported bundle.
