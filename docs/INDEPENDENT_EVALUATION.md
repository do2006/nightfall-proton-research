# Independent Evaluation Request

NightFall Technologies is seeking technically serious third-party evaluation of the public claims in NightFall Proton Public Research Release 1.0.

## What we are asking evaluators to test

- Whether the published black-box evaluation specification is sufficiently falsifiable.
- Whether recurrence, scope enforcement, proof gating, drift handling, quarantine, rollback, and recovery can be tested without access to proprietary source.
- Whether the published evidence claims are stated with appropriate limitations.
- Which additional measurements would make the evidence stronger.

## What we are not asking for

We are not asking evaluators to endorse NightFall Technologies, accept internal results on trust, or sign an NDA merely to inspect the public package. We are also not publishing the proprietary Proton/MCF/CSC implementation as part of this request.

Critical results are welcome. A useful external evaluation should identify evaluator identity/affiliation where appropriate, environment, methodology, fixture hashes, pass/fail criteria, deviations from the published specification, and failures or ambiguities found.
